Frequently asked questions

How do I add a report?

On the Overview screen, tap Upload to choose a PDF or photo, Scan to use your camera, or Manual to type in values. The first time you upload or scan, you will be asked for permission to send the report to Google’s Gemini service for reading.

A value was read incorrectly. What should I do?

AI-based reading can make mistakes. Open the report in Records, compare the extracted values with the original document, and correct the value, or delete the report and re-add it. Please also email us the lab name and what went wrong (without sending personal details) so we can improve.

Which labs and file types work?

Most text-based PDFs and clear photos of printed lab reports work best. Blurry photos, handwriting, or heavily stylized layouts may not be read correctly. If a report can’t be read, use Manual entry.

Where is my data stored?

Your reports and results are stored on your device. When you upload or scan a report, its contents are sent to Google’s Gemini service for parsing. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data. You can also delete individual reports from the Records tab, or delete the app.

Can I stop reports being sent to Google?

Yes. Turn off AI parsing in Settings → Privacy & Data. Upload and scan will be disabled, and you can still add results with Manual entry.

How do I export my results?

Go to Settings → Data Management → Export Health Data.

What do the status colors mean?

Green means a result is within the reference range. Amber means slightly outside it. Red means well outside it. A status is only a comparison against the reference range, not a diagnosis.

Why does my range differ from my lab’s?

Reference ranges vary between labs, ages and sexes. Whenever your report includes its own range, check that against the range shown in the app.

How does the next test reminder work?

Set a reminder interval in Settings. Labwise counts from your most recent test date and shows when the next test is due.

Is Labwise medical advice?

No. It is for information only. Please talk to a doctor about your results.

Troubleshooting

  • Upload or Scan is disabled: AI parsing permission may be turned off. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • A report couldn’t be read: try a clearer photo or the original PDF, or use Manual entry.
  • Face ID not working: check Face ID permission for Labwise in your device Settings.
  • Reminders not arriving: check notification permission for Labwise in your device Settings.
